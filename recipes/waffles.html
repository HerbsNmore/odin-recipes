Strawberry Buttermilk Waffles

Description

Wake up to these delicious, fluffy, strawberry buttermilk waffles. These are easy to make and pair great with a side of bacon or sausage.

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups of all-purpose flour
  • 4 tsp of baking powder
  • 3/4 tsp salt
  • 2 cups of buttermilk
  • 1/2 cup vanilla Greek-style yogurt
  • 1/2 cup butter, melted
  • 2 eggs, beaten
  • 1 1/2 tbsp white sugar
  • 3/4 cup chopped strawberries, or more to taste
  • Optional: whipped cream, softened butter, and/or syrup for topping

Steps

  1. Preheat and grease a waffle iron according to manufacturer's instructions.
  2. In a medium sized bowl, sift flour, baking powder, and salt together. In a large sized bowl, whisk buttermilk, yogurt, butter, eggs, and sugar together. Stir wet ingredients into dry ingredients until batter is smooth. Fold chopped strawberries into batter.
  3. Pour about 1/3 cup batter into preheated waffle iron; cook until lightly browned, about 5 -7 minutes. Repeat with remaining batter.
  4. Optional: top with extra strawberries, whipped cream, softened butter, and/or syrup.
